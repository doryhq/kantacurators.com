Who We Are

Kanta Curators is a Delhi-based brand bringing Indian craftsmanship and thoughtful design together. We curate for people who appreciate objects with character — pieces that carry a story, not just a season.

How We Work

We work closely with skilled artisans across India, many carrying forward generations of craft expertise. Every product is handmade — which means no two pieces are ever exactly the same. That variation isn't a flaw; it's the point.

Our Philosophy

Details matter. We choose thoughtful design over excess, curating pieces that complement modern homes while staying rooted in Indian tradition — made to be cherished for years, not just for one celebration.
