Our story

A creative legacy, carried forward.

Kanta Curators began as a daughter’s tribute to her mother—the creativity she grew up around, the patience behind handmade work, and the belief that thoughtful details make everyday moments memorable. Today, the brand carries that spirit forward through décor and gifting rooted in Indian traditions and designed for modern homes. It celebrates the bond between mothers and daughters, women’s creativity, and the confidence to turn a personal passion into independent work. Each piece is selected or made with care, because the things we welcome into our homes and celebrations should hold meaning beyond the moment.
