Our Story

A daughter’s tribute. A mother’s creative legacy. A celebration of handmade traditions.

Creativity, carried from one generation to the next.

Kanta Curators began with the creativity a daughter witnessed at home: the patience behind handmade work, an instinct for thoughtful details, and the care that turns an object into something personal. What began as admiration became a way to carry that passion forward.

The brand brings Indian craft and celebration into contemporary homes through carefully chosen décor, wedding details, festive gifting and custom creations. It celebrates the bond between mothers and daughters, women’s creative independence, and the value of building meaningful work from a personal legacy.

We do not believe every celebration should look the same. Our role is to help customers find—or create—pieces that feel considered, relevant to the occasion and personal to the people receiving them.

What guides us

Meaning before volume

We prefer thoughtful curation and limited selections over a catalogue filled without purpose.

Tradition, made relevant

Our collections draw from Indian rituals and visual culture while fitting naturally into present-day homes and celebrations.

Personal service

Orders often need a conversation. We help customers discuss colour, quantity, packaging, timing and custom details directly.
