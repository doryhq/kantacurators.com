Care & Warranty

Simple guidance to help preserve handcrafted details, with personal support when you need product-specific advice.

Caring for your piece

  • Store in a clean, dry place away from prolonged moisture.
  • Keep away from extended direct sunlight where possible.
  • Dust gently with a soft, dry cloth.
  • Avoid harsh chemicals and abrasive cleaners.
  • Handle decorative and embellished elements with care.
  • Retain the original packaging for safe storage when possible.

Six-month craftsmanship warranty

Eligible larger pieces include a six-month craftsmanship warranty covering confirmed manufacturing or workmanship defects. Coverage does not include accidental damage, moisture damage, misuse, normal wear, improper storage or unauthorised alterations. Product eligibility and exact coverage are confirmed at the time of purchase.
