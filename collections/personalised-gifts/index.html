Personalised Gifts

Personalised gifts are developed around the recipient and occasion rather than taken from a fixed template. Depending on the piece, customisation can include colours, names or initials, packaging, combinations and presentation. This service is suited to customers looking for wedding, housewarming, festive, milestone or corporate gifts with a more individual finish.

Customisation can be discussed for every design. Tell us what you would like to change—colour, quantity, scale, packaging or personalised details—and we will confirm the best way to adapt it for your requirement.

Frequently asked questions

What can be personalised?

Options vary by product and may include colour, names, initials, packaging or combinations.

Is there a minimum quantity?

Some custom or bulk formats may have minimum quantities. We will confirm this after reviewing your brief.

Can I share a reference image?

Yes. Reference images help communicate a direction, but the final design will depend on available materials and production feasibility.
